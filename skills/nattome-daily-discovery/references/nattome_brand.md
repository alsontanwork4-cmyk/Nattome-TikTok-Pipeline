# Nattome Brand Voice Reference

## What Nattome Is

Nattome is Atomic Group's flagship digestive health brand: a natural, clinically backed gut-care system for Malaysians who want relief now and repair over time.

The core portfolio:

| Product | Role | When the trend signals this product |
|---|---|---|
| **DH** | Daily digestive maintenance | Daily gut care, morning routines, natural ingredients, family-safe habits, prevention, "I wish I started earlier" content |
| **DR** | Faster relief | Reflux, bloating, heartburn, indigestion, gastric discomfort, food regret, post-meal discomfort, "I need comfort now" content |
| **DH-R / Recovery** | Deeper gut repair | Recovery after recurring stomach issues, antibiotics, food poisoning, heavy travel, illness, or gut-lining repair themes |

Public positioning centers on fermented soy, Gastro-AD, natural stomach repair, and the idea that gut health is everyday self-care, not only emergency medicine.

Retail is now a major trust signal. Nattome is available through CARiNG Pharmacy, BIG Pharmacy, Watsons, Wellings, and other pharmacies. In 2026, retail growth has surpassed online growth, with a target path toward 4,000 stores.

## Core Audience Avatars

Pick one primary avatar per angle. Do not write generic "everyone with stomach problems" content.

### The Sufferer

Has reflux, bloating, heartburn, indigestion, or gastric discomfort. Wants relief quickly and may feel anxious, embarrassed, or tired of the same symptoms returning.

Use symptom-led openings:
- "Always bloated after meals?"
- "Heartburn again?"
- "Acid reflux acting up after dinner?"
- "That uncomfortable gastric feeling again?"

Lead with **DR** for faster relief, then bridge to DH or DH-R when the angle is about ongoing care.

### The Maintainer

Wants daily gut care, natural ingredients, family-safe routines, and long-term repair instead of repeat antacid use.

Use routine-led openings:
- "If gut care is part of your morning routine..."
- "One simple daily habit for your stomach..."
- "Taking care of your gut before discomfort starts..."

Lead with **DH** and emphasize daily digestive maintenance, gentle ingredients, fermented soy, and clinical credibility.

### The Pharmacy Browser

Notices Nattome in CARiNG, BIG, Watsons, Wellings, or through pharmacist recommendation. Needs fast proof and a simple product choice.

Use retail-led openings:
- "Saw Nattome at the pharmacy and not sure which one to choose?"
- "If you spotted this at CARiNG or BIG..."
- "Pharmacy shelf, stomach discomfort, and too many choices?"

Make the product choice simple: DR for faster relief, DH for daily care, DH-R/recovery for deeper repair.

### The Family Caregiver

Buys for parents, spouse, children, or older relatives. Responds to care-led messaging such as "care for their stomach," Parents' Day themes, and "health is the best gift."

Use family-led openings:
- "If your parents always complain about gastric discomfort..."
- "A simple way to care for your family's stomach..."
- "Health is still the best gift for the people you love."

Pair emotional care with practical product choice. Avoid guilt or fearmongering.

### The Concerned Preventer

Sees stomach issues as a warning sign. Responds to education, risk factors, clinical data, and "start before it gets worse."

Use education-led openings:
- "Relying on antacids too often?"
- "Frequent bloating is your sign to care for your gut daily."
- "Don't wait until stomach discomfort becomes your routine."

Be calm and practical. Do not use extreme disease fear or imply cancer prevention.

## Messaging Pillars

### 1. Relief Plus Repair

DR is for immediate discomfort and faster relief. DH is for daily care and gut-lining support. DH-R/recovery is for deeper gut repair.

Good copy:
- "For today, you want comfort. For the long term, your stomach needs care."
- "DR helps when discomfort hits. DH helps make gut care a daily habit."

Avoid making the brand feel like only a short-term painkiller.

### 2. Natural Vs Chemical

Position Nattome against antacid-only habits and Gaviscon-style temporary relief, but avoid aggressive competitor attacks unless legally approved.

Nattome's territory:
- Natural digestive care
- Fermented soy
- No unnecessary chemicals
- Relief plus longer-term repair
- Everyday stomach protection

Acceptable framing:
- "Antacids can help temporarily, but your stomach also needs daily care."
- "Instead of only reacting when discomfort hits, build a natural daily gut-care routine."

Avoid:
- "Gaviscon is bad."
- "Throw away your antacids."
- "Nattome beats Gaviscon."

### 3. Clinical Credibility

Cite exact substantiated clinical references only:
- UCSI
- ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06524271
- Exact substantiated outcomes only when the source is available and verified

Use:
- "Clinically studied"
- "Shown in study"
- "A gentle daily routine, backed by clinical study"

Avoid:
- "Science-backed" as a vague standalone claim
- "Clinically proven to cure"
- "Doctor recommended" unless specifically approved
- Any made-up percentages or outcomes

### 4. Easy Daily Habit

Make Nattome feel easy to adopt:
- One cup or sachet
- Simple to mix
- Portable
- Suitable for busy mornings
- Fits family routines

Good copy:
- "One simple cup before your day starts."
- "Easy to keep at home, in your bag, or at the office."

### 5. Retail Trust

Retail availability is a proof point, not just a CTA.

Use:
- CARiNG Pharmacy
- BIG Pharmacy
- Watsons
- Wellings
- Other pharmacies
- Pharmacist recommendation when applicable
- "Visible offline, not just another online health ad"

Good copy:
- "You can find Nattome at pharmacies like CARiNG, BIG, Watsons, and Wellings."
- "If you saw it on the pharmacy shelf, here's the simple way to choose."

### 6. Family Care

Use care-led messaging:
- "Protect your parents' stomach"
- "Daily gut care for the people you love"
- "Health is the best gift"
- "Care for their stomach before discomfort becomes routine"

Avoid guilt-heavy framing or implying the buyer is neglectful.

## Tone Rules

The voice should be warm but not childish, educational but not academic, direct but not fearmongering.

Open with symptoms, then move quickly to:

1. The natural mechanism
2. The relevant product choice
3. Clinical proof where appropriate
4. Pharmacy or WhatsApp CTA

English copy should be cleaner, more credible, and less hard-sell. Chinese copy can be more conversational, family-led, and emotionally warm.

Use simple health language:
- Acid reflux
- Bloating
- Heartburn
- Indigestion
- Gut lining
- Inflammation
- Daily repair
- Stomach protection

Pair emotional care with proof:
- "A gentle daily routine, backed by clinical study."
- "Natural stomach care that fits into everyday Malaysian life."

## What To Do

- Use symptom-led openings.
- Name the specific product: DH, DR, or DH-R/recovery.
- Say why that product fits the moment.
- Pick one avatar per angle.
- Connect emotional care with clinical credibility.
- Mention pharmacy availability when the angle needs trust.
- Keep claims specific, substantiated, and calm.
- Make the CTA simple: pharmacy, WhatsApp, or product choice.

## What Not To Do

- Do not promise cures.
- Do not claim cancer prevention.
- Do not promise zero side effects.
- Do not guarantee medical outcomes.
- Do not overuse scare tactics like "stomach cancer is waiting for you."
- Do not sound like generic supplement advertising.
- Do not rely only on awards or discounts.
- Do not make the brand feel like a short-term painkiller.
- Do not use vague clinical language when a specific citation is needed.
- Do not aggressively attack competitors unless legally approved.

## Format Judgment: Talking Head Vs VLOG Vs Both

Nattome uses talking head, VLOG, and hybrid formats. Pick based on the trend.

**Talking head works when:**
- The angle is educational.
- The angle explains reflux, bloating, gut lining, or repair.
- Clinical credibility needs to be delivered clearly.
- The product choice needs a simple explanation.

**VLOG works when:**
- The angle is a relatable daily moment.
- The story involves pharmacy browsing, family care, meals, travel, or morning routine.
- The avatar is the Maintainer, Pharmacy Browser, or Family Caregiver.

**Both works when:**
- The video has a setup-payoff structure.
- VLOG opens with the symptom or family moment.
- Talking head explains the mechanism, product choice, and study proof.

This hybrid format is often strongest for Nattome because it combines emotional recognition with credibility.
